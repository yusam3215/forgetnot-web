The "Parse & Discard" Privacy Guarantee

ForgetNot does not harvest, store, or sell your documents. Incoming screenshots and forwarded renewal emails are processed in transient server memory solely to extract deadline dates, immediately discarded from volatile memory, and saved exclusively to your local device vault.

1. Information We Collect and Process

ForgetNot is built with a zero-retention philosophy for personal files. Here is what happens when you use our services:

  • Screenshot & Image Extraction: When you share an image with ForgetNot, it is converted into an encrypted base64 payload and transmitted via secure TLS/HTTPS to our parsing pipeline. The model extracts the title, cutoff date, and category, and returns structured metadata. The image binary is immediately discarded from server memory.
  • Inbound Email Forwarding: If you use your dedicated inbound forwarding address (e.g., yourname@inbound.forgetnot.app), emails are processed transiently via secure webhooks to identify renewal dates and reminder criteria. Raw email contents are not archived in permanent databases.
  • Local Storage: All saved items, reminders, and category classifications are stored directly on your physical smartphone using local encrypted device storage (AsyncStorage).

2. Third-Party Infrastructure

We work with trusted industry providers solely to deliver app functionality:

  • RevenueCat: Handles entitlement tracking, receipt validation, and subscription status synchronization. RevenueCat does not store your financial payment instruments.
  • Google Play & Apple App Store: All financial transactions, billing details, and payment credentials are processed directly by Google and Apple.
  • Resend: Provides inbound webhook routing for receipt forwarding.

3. Subscriptions, Purchases, and Cancellations

ForgetNot offers both free tier functionality (up to 3 saved vault items) and premium access through ForgetNot Pro:

  • Monthly Subscription ($2.99 / month): Automatically renews each month until canceled. Payment is charged to your App Store or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Annual Subscription ($12.99 / year): Includes an optional 7-day free trial. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours prior to the conclusion of the 7-day trial period, your account will be charged $12.99. Renews annually.
  • Lifetime Access ($59.99 one-time purchase): A single upfront payment granting permanent, non-expiring access to ForgetNot Pro features without recurring renewals or future subscription charges.
  • Cancellation Policy: You may cancel recurring subscriptions at any time through your Google Play Store account settings (under "Payments & Subscriptions") or your Apple ID Subscriptions menu. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

4. Data Deletion and User Rights

Because your vault items are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete individual vault items directly from the Vault screen at any time.
  • Deleting the app or clearing application storage removes all vault entries permanently from your hardware.
  • To revoke your inbound email handle or request account deregistration, use the "Delete Account" option in the Settings tab.
